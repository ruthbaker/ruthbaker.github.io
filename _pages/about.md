I am a Professor of Applied Mathematics at the Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford, and a Tutorial Fellow in Mathematics at St Hugh’s College. I am a member of the [Wolfson Centre for Mathematical Biology](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/groups/mathematical-biology) and the [Machine Learning and Data Science Group](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/groups/ml-and-ds), and I am also affiliated with the [Institute for Developmental and Regenerative Medicine](https://www.idrm.ox.ac.uk). I am a [Simons Investigator, Theoretical Physics in Life Sciences](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/grant/simons-investigators/?tab=awardees).
